# Addition of Two Integers
## The addition of 2 whole numbers (integers) depends on what sign values they take
### **EXAMPLE 1: Same signs**

When the numbers are the same sign, you add the absolute values of the numbers and then keep the original sign (|1|+|1|=|2|=2)

### **Example 2: Different signs** 

When the numbers have different signs and subtract the smaller absolute value from the bigger absolute value number and use the sign of the number with the bigger absolute value (|-3|-|2|=3-2=1=-1)

### **Example 3: Adding to Zero (clone example)**

$2-2=0$

### **Example 1: Multiplying two integers**

2*2 = 4
