# Multiplication of Two Integers

Multiplication finds the product of two integers. For a positive multiplier, it can be understood as repeated addition.

## Sign Rules

- Two integers with the same sign have a positive product.
- Two integers with different signs have a negative product.
- Any integer multiplied by zero gives zero.

## Example 1: Positive Integers

`4 × 3 = 12`

Adding 4 three times gives `4 + 4 + 4 = 12`.

## Example 2: Different Signs

`(-4) × 3 = -12`

Adding -4 three times gives `(-4) + (-4) + (-4) = -12`. The signs differ, so the product is negative.

## Example 3: Two Negative Integers

`(-4) × (-3) = 12`

The absolute values multiply to `4 × 3 = 12`. Both factors are negative, so their product is positive.
